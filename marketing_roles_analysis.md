# Marketing Roles Analysis for CRM Expansion

Based on the analysis of the CRM product and the existing marketing strategy, here are the recommended marketing roles to report to the CMO for team expansion:

## 1. Growth Hacker

**Primary Responsibilities:**
- Execute the 30-day go-to-market strategy outlined in marketing_strategy.md
- Manage cold email campaigns and LinkedIn outreach
- Track and optimize conversion rates from outreach to demos to customers
- Implement and manage referral programs
- Analyze marketing metrics and adjust strategies weekly
- Manage partnerships with joyero associations and other B2B channels
- Oversee potential paid advertising campaigns (LinkedIn Ads, Google Ads)

**Required Skills:**
- Experience with B2B SaaS growth strategies
- Proficiency with cold email tools (Hunter.io, LinkedIn Sales Navigator)
- LinkedIn organic growth and engagement tactics
- Basic analytics (Google Sheets, Bitly, LinkedIn Analytics)
- Understanding of CRM sales cycles and B2B conversion funnels
- Ability to create and optimize landing pages and lead magnets

**KPIs:**
- Weekly: Number of personalized outreach emails sent
- Bi-weekly: Response rate and demo booking rate
- Monthly: Number of pilot customers acquired
- Quarterly: Customer acquisition cost (CAC) and lifetime value (LTV)

## 2. Content Marketing Specialist / Copywriter

**Primary Responsibilities:**
- Create educational content for LinkedIn posts and articles
- Develop lead magnets (checklists, calculators, guides)
- Write persuasive cold email templates and follow-up sequences
- Create video scripts for product demos and testimonials
- Develop case studies from early pilot customers
- Optimize content for SEO (CRM for joyerías, WhatsApp Business CRM)
- Manage content calendar and ensure consistent publishing

**Required Skills:**
- Strong copywriting and storytelling abilities
- Experience creating B2B educational content
- Basic graphic design skills (Canva) for visual content
- Video scripting and basic video production understanding
- SEO knowledge for B2B content
- Ability to translate technical features into benefit-driven messaging
- Understanding of luxury jewelry/watch industry pain points

**KPIs:**
- Weekly: Number of content pieces published
- Bi-weekly: Engagement rates on LinkedIn content
- Monthly: Lead magnet download rates
- Quarterly: Content-influenced pipeline value

## Implementation Timeline

**Month 1:**
- Hire both roles simultaneously to execute the 30-day GTM strategy
- Growth Hacker focuses on outreach and prospect identification
- Content Specialist creates launch content and lead magnets
- Both collaborate on launching the cold email campaign

**Month 2-3:**
- Growth Hacker optimizes outreach based on Month 1 results
- Content Specialist creates case studies from pilot customers
- Both prepare for potential paid advertising if budget becomes available
- Begin planning for webinar series and partnership development

These roles directly support the objectives outlined in the parent issue CDC-6 and will enable the CMO to scale marketing efforts beyond individual execution to a sustainable, repeatable growth engine for the CRM product.
